# Retained tensor views

[Affine rebasing](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/affine.py) projects an alias's logical addresses into its producer's physical layout. [Bufferization](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/bufferize.py) and [Graph/Kernel correspondence](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/graph_to_kernel.py) share this proof. [Address enumeration and rejection tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_affine_rebase.py) cover broadcasts, offsets, strided slices and layout boundaries.

`STRIDED_COPY` accepts an affine source view; its destination retains a consumer layout. The [Python](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/ir/kernel_verify.py) and [native](../../src/dev/ai_mesh/mesh_ir_physical_operation_verifier.cc) readers enforce this contract. [Export-to-Garnet tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_retained_broadcast.py) cover GQA replication at TP1/TP4 and reject corrupted strides and untyped destinations.

[Placement](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/placement.py) evaluates the complete configured candidate set and retains the selected core group's strategy alternatives. [Equivalence tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_placement_evaluation.py) check exact selection against direct geometry evaluation. Pass statistics distinguish evaluated candidates from retained records; [execution records](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/execution.py) own completion timings.

[Geometry evaluation](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/placement_evaluation.py) distributes independent templates through the shared process pool. [Parallel search tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_parallel_placement.py) cover complete candidate scores and rejection counts at 2 and 32 workers; [backend determinism](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_gate2_backend_compiler.py) checks canonical artifacts and encoded bytes. Worker counts bound compiler concurrency independently of physical model placement. Operation ledger transitions and single-variant movement emission retain their ordering.

[Local shard reuse](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/placement_geometry.py) and [matrix contraction tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_retained_matrix_contraction.py) cover retained inputs with different producer and consumer partitions. The [query/key carrier](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_retained_qk.py) exercises the complete backend and native AXI/Garnet execution in FP16 and FP32.

[Consumer layout projection](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/affine.py) is shared by placement and bufferization. [Retained transpose tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_retained_elementwise.py) cover owner-local materialization; [broadcast projection](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/regions.py) and [native RMS tests](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_elementwise_broadcast.py) cover physical elementwise inputs. Runtime result-read classification uses admitted geometry in [the core](../../src/dev/ai_mesh/mesh_dummy_core.cc).

[Dependency queries](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/dependency.py), [exact byte access frontiers](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/access.py) and [immutable verification certificates](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/ir/kernel_ir.py) share ordering proofs across validation and allocation. [Reachability oracles](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_dependency_search.py), [partial-access oracles](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_access_frontiers.py) and [bounded-memory regression](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_dependency_query_memory.py) cover correctness and scaling.

[Movement window lifetimes](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_movement_window_lifetime.py) and [native streamed weight reuse](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/integration/test_streamed_weight_reuse.py) cover operation-boundary dependencies and strict SRAM allocation. [Real-prefix repair evidence](../../.tmp/docs/qwen3-8b-smoke/stage-b/verification-allocation-result.json) indexes validation and the full-model run.

[Command reservations](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/passes/command_reservations.py), [capacity admission](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/command_capacity.py) and [placement carriers](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_command_capacity_planning.py) cover cumulative per-core ROM budgets. [Derivation reuse](../../util/mesh_ir/mesh_ir/analysis/derivation.py), [cached digest checks](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_scheduled_derivation_reuse.py) and [descriptor index checks](../../util/mesh_ir/tests/unit/test_descriptor_address_index.py) cover immutable validation and lookup scaling.
